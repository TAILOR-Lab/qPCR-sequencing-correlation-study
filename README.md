# qPCR Sequencing Correlation Study

This repository contains the code, data, and analysis pipelines for the study titled:

Four Years of Continuous Weekly Wastewater Analysis Reveals Sequencing as a Robust Alternative to PCR for Respiratory Virome Surveillance.

Authors: Harihara Prakash, Ryan K. Perez, Matt Ross, Michael Tisza, Juwan Cormier, Vasanthi Avadhanula, Pedro A Piedra, Justin R. Clark, Anthony W. Maresso

Author for Correspondence: <Dr. Justin R. Clark>

This codebase is under active development and may change over time.

In this study, we develop a comparative framework between qPCR and sequencing of 5 known respiratory viruses in wastewater samples collected over 4 years of sampling across Texas. We analyzed samples from over 16 cities and 40 sites across Texas, and assessed the correlation between qPCR abundances with hybrid-capture sequencing abundances of the 5 viruses.
